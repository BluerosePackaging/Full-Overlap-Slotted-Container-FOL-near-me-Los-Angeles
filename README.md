## Built Around Added Flap Coverage

The defining feature of an FOL box is its flap design. Unlike standard boxes where the flaps meet at the center, the longer flaps completely overlap one another. This creates additional layers of corrugated material across the top and bottom of the container.

That extra coverage can help distribute pressure and provide additional protection when packages are stacked, moved, or transported. It makes FOL boxes particularly useful for applications where ordinary carton construction may not provide enough reinforcement.
